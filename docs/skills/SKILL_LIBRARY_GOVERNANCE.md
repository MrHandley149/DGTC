## Purpose

This document governs the creation, change, use, and retirement of DGTC competence roles.

## Role Criteria and Count

A competence role must have an Approved role document, a defined subject-matter scope, boundaries, review responsibilities, and an entry in the [Skill Library Index](SKILL_LIBRARY_INDEX.md). The index is the only authoritative role count.

Operational processes, tools, agents, workflows, committees, and project functions are not competence roles unless separately approved and registered. Git Operations is therefore excluded.

## Authority

Competence roles analyze, advise, challenge, and cross-review within their documented scope. They do not set project priority, accept risk, approve governance, or replace the Product Owner. Material role creation, mandate change, or retirement requires Product Owner approval and synchronized updates to the index, orchestration model, manifest, register, and affected cross-references.

Git Operations may execute and verify the repository mechanics for an approved role change, but cannot approve the role or its content.

## Reality and Cross-Review

Material multidisciplinary recommendations require review by all materially affected registered roles. Where evidence quality or material uncertainty is relevant, orchestration must include a Verklighetskontroll or equivalent evidence function. Review findings remain advisory until accepted by the authorized decision owner.
