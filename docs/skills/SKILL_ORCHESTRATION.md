# DGTC Skill Orchestration

**Document Type:** Competence Workflow  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft — Ready for Product Owner Approval  
**Information Owner:** Project

---

## Purpose

This workflow determines which competence roles participate, in what order, what each produces, and how advice reaches the Product Owner without confusing consultation with authority.

## Operating Model

```text
Governed question
    -> Decision owner and scope
    -> Lead role selected
    -> Required roles triggered
    -> Independent analyses
    -> Cross-role challenge
    -> Consolidated recommendation
    -> Verklighetskontroll when required
    -> Product Owner decision
    -> Documentation and traceability
```

The Guardian or Program Manager coordinates the process. Neither may manufacture consensus, suppress material disagreement, or decide on behalf of the Product Owner.

## Selection Rules

Use the smallest set of roles that covers material effects. A role is mandatory when the work changes or makes claims within its owned perspective.

| Change or question | Lead | Mandatory consultation |
|---|---|---|
| Positioning, campaign, audience promise | Marknadsförare | Vardagsspelare; Verklighetscontroller for external claims |
| Sales model, pipeline, price-to-sale process | Försäljningschef | Marknadsförare; Sponsoransvarig if partners are involved |
| Sponsorship package or activation | Sponsoransvarig | Försäljningschef, Föreningsordförande, Verklighetscontroller |
| Elite feature or performance claim | Elitspelare | Tävlingsspelare, Verklighetscontroller; Technical Architect if built |
| Mainstream workflow or engagement design | Vardagsspelare | Marknadsförare; UX specialist when available |
| Competition workflow | Tävlingsspelare | Elitspelare, Vardagsspelare |
| Club deployment or partnership | Föreningsordförande | Vardagsspelare, Sponsoransvarig, Operations Manager |
| Material uncertainty or readiness | Verklighetscontroller | Every role materially affected; supporting specialists as required |

## Mandatory Verklighetskontroll Triggers

A Verklighetskontroll is required before recommending work that includes:

- external market, user, performance, financial, or operational claims;
- strategic investment or significant irreversible cost;
- sponsor or institutional commitments;
- safety, privacy, legal, safeguarding, or reputational exposure;
- material disagreement between competence roles;
- a Go / Conditional Go / No Go readiness recommendation;
- an assumption whose failure would invalidate the preferred option;
- evidence distorted by optimism, confirmation bias, selection bias, or survivorship bias.

## Verklighetsrapport

The Verklighetscontroller produces or assures a report containing:

1. question and decision owner;
2. verified evidence and source quality;
3. indications;
4. hypotheses and falsification tests;
5. assumptions;
6. data gaps, owners, impact, and resolution path;
7. downside, operational, ethical, and reputational risks;
8. alternative explanations and minority views;
9. role findings and unresolved conflicts;
10. recommendation with conditions;
11. Go / Conditional Go / No Go when requested;
12. Product Owner decisions required.

## Review Protocol

Roles analyze independently before group consolidation to reduce anchoring. Each review records:

- concerns within the reviewer's competence;
- missing evidence;
- dependency or boundary problems;
- concrete improvement;
- severity: critical, material, minor, or observation;
- disposition: accepted, partly accepted, rejected with reason, or Data Gap.

Program Manager consolidation is administrative and analytical, not approval. The lead role remains accountable for the quality of its advice. The Product Owner makes the decision.

## Conflict Resolution

1. Identify whether disagreement concerns evidence, interpretation, values, scope, or risk appetite.
2. Resolve evidence conflicts through authoritative sources or a bounded experiment.
3. Preserve competing interpretations when evidence is insufficient.
4. Escalate scope, priority, commercial commitment, architecture, or risk acceptance to the Product Owner.
5. Never use voting to disguise missing authority or evidence.

## Handoffs

A role hands off when the primary question crosses its competence boundary. Handoffs state what is known, what is unknown, what decision is pending, and why the receiving role is needed. Responsibility is not transferred silently.

## Outputs and Traceability

Every orchestration record identifies:

- task or decision reference;
- consulted roles and reasons;
- sources;
- evidence classification;
- recommendations and dissent;
- Verklighetsrapport link when triggered;
- Product Owner decision;
- affected documents;
- validation and follow-up.

## Quality Gates

Orchestration is complete only when scope, authority, required consultations, evidence classes, conflicts, boundaries, risks, recommendations, and traceability are explicit. Missing mandatory consultation blocks a final recommendation but does not prevent discovery work.
