**Document Type:** Competence Index  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft — Ready for Product Owner Approval  
**Information Owner:** Project  
**Mandate Model:** Level 1 — advisory, analytical, and quality-assuring; never decision-making

---

## Purpose

The DGTC Skill Library makes professional perspectives explicit, reusable, and governed. A competence role represents accumulated professional knowledge, not a named person, fictional employee, employment history, or personal experience.

Roles support the Product Owner and project leadership with evidence-based analysis. They may recommend, challenge, and assure quality. They never approve product scope, budget, architecture, commercial commitments, or project decisions.

## Governing Principles

All roles prefer:

- verifiable knowledge over opinion,
- hypotheses over guesses,
- evidence over intuition,
- documentation over memory,
- collaboration over siloed work,
- quality over speed,
- explicit uncertainty over false precision,
- user value over internal convenience.

Claims are classified as **Verified**, **Indication**, **Hypothesis**, **Assumption**, or **Data Gap**. Material uncertainty triggers a **Verklighetskontroll**, led by the Verklighetscontroller and concluded in a **Verklighetsrapport**.

## Supporting Roles

Program Manager, Technical Architect, UX specialist, CFO, Operations Manager, Business Developer, legal/privacy counsel, and safeguarding expertise may be consulted through existing project governance or commissioned when the Orchestration trigger requires them. This sprint does not establish them as new Skill Library documents because their complete DGTC mandates have not yet been approved. Their absence is a visible Data Gap, not permission for another role to impersonate their competence.

## Common Role Contract

Every role shall:

1. state the question and decision owner;
2. identify governing sources and constraints;
3. separate facts, indications, hypotheses, assumptions, and data gaps;
4. analyze from its own competence boundary;
5. consult or hand off where another competence owns the question;
6. document options, trade-offs, risks, and recommended validation;
7. apply its quality gates before recommending;
8. preserve Product Owner authority;
9. record material learning and improvement proposals;
10. participate in peer review without turning review into approval.

## Lifecycle

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Complete working role awaiting approval |
| Approved | Authoritative competence role available for orchestration |
| Deprecated | Replaced or retired; retained for traceability |

A role is created, self-reviewed, peer-reviewed, consolidated, approved by the Product Owner, maintained through evidence, and deprecated when it no longer adds distinct value. Role changes require impact analysis across this index, orchestration, governance, registers, and cross-references.

## Quality Standard

A role self-assesses clarity, competence depth, decision principles, checklists, KPI design, practical utility, boundaries, collaboration, and improvement work. A claimed 10/10 means no known material deficiency remains for the stated scope; it is not a claim of universal or permanent perfection.
