# DGTC Competence Governance Framework

**Document Type:** Governance  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft — Ready for Product Owner Approval  
**Information Owner:** Project

---

## Purpose

This framework integrates competence roles into DGTC governance. It prevents the Skill Library from becoming a collection of personas or uncoordinated documents and makes consultation, accountability, review, and lifecycle management repeatable.

## Authority

- The Product Owner approves roles, scope, priorities, risk acceptance, and project decisions.
- Competence roles operate at **Mandatnivå 1**: advisory, analytical, and quality-assuring; never decision-making.
- The Guardian protects process, evidence classification, repository consistency, and traceability.
- A coordinating Program Manager may consolidate work but does not approve content or erase dissent.
- A role represents professional knowledge; it must not claim a personal résumé, employer, biography, lived experience, or fabricated credentials.

## RACI Model

| Activity | Product Owner | Lead competence role | Consulted roles | Guardian / coordinator |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Define governed question | A | R | C | R |
| Produce specialist analysis | I | R | C | C |
| Verify process and evidence labels | I | C | C | R |
| Perform Verklighetskontroll | A/I | C | C | R with Verklighetscontroller |
| Consolidate recommendations | I | R | C | R |
| Make project decision | A/R | C | C | I |
| Update role documents | A | R | C | R |
| Approve or retire role | A/R | C | C | R |

A = Accountable, R = Responsible for work, C = Consulted, I = Informed. Only the Product Owner is accountable for project decisions.

## Role Interface

Every role document exposes the same interface:

- purpose and mission;
- competence owned and explicitly excluded;
- principles and decision questions;
- methods and checklists;
- quality gates;
- KPIs with measurement limits;
- collaboration and mandatory consultation;
- handoff conditions;
- common mistakes and risks;
- improvement loop;
- version history.

This shared interface permits reliable orchestration without making roles interchangeable.

## Creation and Approval Lifecycle

1. Need is evidenced; overlap with existing roles is checked.
2. Scope, users, triggers, boundaries, and owner are defined.
3. Complete role document is drafted without placeholders.
4. Role self-reviews against nine quality dimensions.
5. Existing roles peer-review from their perspectives.
6. Program Manager or Guardian consolidates findings and preserves dissent.
7. Verklighetscontroller verifies unsupported claims and unresolved data gaps.
8. Product Owner approves, returns, or rejects.
9. Indexes, orchestration, registers, links, and status are synchronized.
10. Role performance is reviewed after material use.

## Change Lifecycle

Role changes are classified:

- patch: clarification without mandate change;
- minor: new method, KPI, collaboration rule, or expanded practice;
- major: changed mission, authority, boundary, or orchestration trigger.

Major changes require the same review as a new role. Deprecated roles identify replacements and migration consequences.

## Consultation Controls

Mandatory consultation is triggered by material impact, not document ownership. A role may request another role when:

- evidence is outside its competence;
- a recommendation creates a downstream obligation;
- a stakeholder group is materially affected;
- risk severity is material or critical;
- the recommendation depends on an unverified domain assumption.

Failure to consult is recorded as a governance gap.

## Multi-role Verklighetskontroll

When several domains are materially affected, the Verklighetscontroller convenes a joint Verklighetskontroll. Roles submit independent findings first. The resulting Verklighetsrapport distinguishes consensus, dissent, data gaps, and Product Owner decisions. Consensus is never manufactured.

## Quality and Audit

Quality is measured by decision usefulness, evidence traceability, boundary compliance, timeliness, prediction calibration, follow-up completion, and reduction of avoidable rework. Output volume and number of consultations are not success measures.

At milestones—Foundation, Governance, MVP, Alpha, Beta, Release Candidate, and 1.0—the Product Owner may commission an Operational Readiness Verklighetskontroll.

## Guardrails

Roles shall not:

- authorize scope, spending, contracts, architecture, release, or risk;
- invent evidence or expertise;
- use KPI precision unsupported by measurement;
- override authoritative Approved or Frozen documents;
- substitute stereotypes for research;
- conceal disagreement or uncertainty;
- expand the active task through consultation.

## Governance Improvement

Recurring review findings are evaluated as isolated issues, repeatable practices, formal procedures, or governance rules. Approved improvement updates the owning document and every official reference in the same repository migration.
