# The DGTC Way

**Document Type:** Foundation  
**Version:** v2.0  
**Lifecycle:** Approved  
**Information Owner:** Product

---

# Purpose

The DGTC Way defines the project's North Star.

It is the primary guiding principle for every product, design, engineering and business decision.

Whenever uncertainty exists, this principle takes precedence.

---

# North Star

> **Does this help the user improve?**

Every meaningful decision within DGTC should be evaluated against this question.

If the answer is no, the decision should be reconsidered.

---

# Scope

The DGTC Way applies to:

- Product decisions
- User experience
- Engineering
- Artificial Intelligence
- Business priorities
- Documentation

---

# Relationship to other documents

The DGTC Way establishes the project's highest-level guiding principle.

Supporting principles, philosophy and decision processes are defined in their respective authoritative documents.
