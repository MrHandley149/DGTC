# AI Research

## Purpose

This directory contains research related to artificial intelligence that may influence the future development of DGTC.

The purpose is to collect knowledge, evaluate emerging technologies, and document findings before they become product requirements, architecture decisions, or implementation work.

Research documents are exploratory and are not considered authoritative project decisions.

## Scope

Topics may include:

- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- AI-assisted coaching
- Explainable AI
- Recommendation systems
- AI-assisted development
- Prompt engineering
- AI ethics
- Human-AI interaction within DGTC
