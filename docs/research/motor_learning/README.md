# Motor Learning Research

## Purpose

This directory contains research related to motor learning and skill acquisition that may influence the future development of DGTC.

The purpose is to collect evidence, evaluate motor learning principles, and document findings before they become coaching methods, product requirements, or implementation work.

Research documents are exploratory and are not considered authoritative project decisions.

## Scope

Topics may include:

- Motor learning theory
- Skill acquisition
- Deliberate practice
- Implicit and explicit learning
- Variable and random practice
- Feedback and knowledge of results
- Transfer and retention
- Performance under pressure
