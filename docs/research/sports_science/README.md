# Sports Science Research

## Purpose

This directory contains research related to sports science that may influence the future development of DGTC.

The purpose is to collect evidence, evaluate sports science principles, and document findings before they become product requirements, coaching methods, or implementation work.

Research documents are exploratory and are not considered authoritative project decisions.

## Scope

Topics may include:

- Exercise physiology
- Biomechanics
- Performance analysis
- Physical conditioning
- Recovery
- Injury prevention
- Training load management
- Performance testing
