# Research

## Purpose

This directory contains research that informs the long-term development of DGTC.

Research documents collect evidence, theories, literature, and exploratory findings before they become product requirements, architecture decisions, or implementation work.

Research supports informed decision-making but is not itself authoritative product or engineering documentation.

## Research Areas

| Area | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `ai/` | Artificial intelligence, AI-assisted development, and intelligent coaching. |
| `hci/` | Human-Computer Interaction, usability, accessibility, and UX. |
| `learning/` | Learning science and educational theory. |
| `motor_learning/` | Motor learning, skill acquisition, and deliberate practice. |
| `psycology/` | Psychology, motivation, confidence, and mental performance. |
| `sports_science/` | Exercise science, biomechanics, recovery, and performance. |

## Principles

Research should:

- Be evidence-informed.
- Clearly distinguish evidence from opinion.
- Cite original sources whenever practical.
- Support future product and architectural decisions.
- Remain independent of implementation.

Research does not automatically become product direction.
