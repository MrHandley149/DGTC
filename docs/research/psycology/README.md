# Psychology Research

## Purpose

This directory contains research related to psychology that may influence the future development of DGTC.

The purpose is to collect evidence, evaluate psychological principles, and document findings before they become product requirements, coaching methods, or implementation work.

Research documents are exploratory and are not considered authoritative project decisions.

## Scope

Topics may include:

- Motivation
- Confidence
- Self-efficacy
- Goal setting
- Mental performance
- Decision making
- Habit formation
- Attention and focus
- Performance under pressure
