# Learning Research

## Purpose

This directory contains research related to learning science and educational theory that may influence the future development of DGTC.

The purpose is to collect evidence, evaluate learning models, and document findings before they become product requirements, coaching methods, or implementation work.

Research documents are exploratory and are not considered authoritative project decisions.

## Scope

Topics may include:

- Motor learning
- Skill acquisition
- Deliberate practice
- Feedback strategies
- Cognitive learning theories
- Motivation and engagement
- Retention and transfer of learning
- Coaching methodologies
