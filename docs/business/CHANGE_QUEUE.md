**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Active

**Document Type:** Governance Document

---

# Purpose

The Business Change Queue captures ideas, improvement proposals, and future documentation identified during the development of the Business Operating System.

Its purpose is to preserve valuable ideas without disrupting the current strategic focus.

An item appearing in the Change Queue does **not** represent an approved decision.

The queue exists to ensure ideas are reviewed deliberately rather than implemented impulsively.

---

# Principles

Whenever a potential improvement is identified, it should follow a consistent process.

1. Record the idea in the Business Change Queue.
2. Provide a short rationale.
3. Review and prioritize it during the appropriate planning cycle.
4. Record the outcome in the authoritative DGTC Decision Log only after an explicit decision has been made.

The Change Queue collects possibilities.

The DGTC Decision Log records decisions until an explicitly approved business-specific decision log exists.

# Status Definitions

| Status | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **New** | Newly identified idea awaiting review. |
| **Under Review** | Currently being evaluated. |
| **Approved** | Accepted for future implementation. |
| **Rejected** | Reviewed and intentionally declined. |
| **Implemented** | Completed and removed from the active queue. |

# Prioritization Principles

Items should be evaluated based on:

- Strategic importance
- Alignment with the Foundation
- Long-term business value
- Impact on communication consistency
- Implementation effort
- Dependencies on other Business documents

High-impact ideas should not automatically receive immediate priority.

Maintaining strategic coherence remains more important than rapid expansion.

---

# Relationship to Other Documents

The Change Queue supports—but does not replace—the formal governance process.

```text
Idea
    │
    ▼
Business Change Queue
    │
    ▼
Strategic Review
    │
    ▼
DGTC Decision Log
    │
    ▼
Documentation Updates
```

Only approved changes should influence the Business Operating System.

---

# Governance

The Change Queue should be reviewed regularly during strategic planning.

Items may remain in the queue indefinitely until sufficient information exists to support a decision.

Removing an item from the queue always requires one of the following outcomes:

- Approved
- Rejected
- Implemented

Ideas should never disappear without traceability.
